Genetic Algorithm (GA) Mechanisms in Games

1. Smart Rockets (Vector DNA)

The chromosome is an array of directional force vectors Force[300] applied per frame. Rockets getting closest or reaching the target faster obtain higher fitness. Crossover blends trajectory patterns from two parent rockets, allowing generations to organically evolve navigating around obstacles.

2. 2D Physics Rover (Morphology DNA)

The chromosome directly encodes vehicle anatomy modeled with 2D rigid-body physics (Matter.js): chassis polygon vertices, front & rear wheel diameters, and wheel axle mount locations. Fitness equals horizontal distance covered along rugged terrain. Overturned or stuck rovers are filtered out, passing resilient geometry to progeny.

Selection

Chooses candidate parents weighted by fitness scores to pass favorable traits ahead.

Crossover

Recombines gene sequences from paired parents to form novel hybrid phenotypes.

Mutation

Randomly perturbs genes to explore undiscovered solutions and avoid local optima.
