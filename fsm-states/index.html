State Legend (from Monster.cs C# code)

IDLE — Cat patrols waypoints. Chases when mouse is close.
CHASE — Cat runs toward mouse. Collides to kill.
EVADE — Cat runs away from mouse (when powered).
DIE — Collision or out of bounds. Respawn after delay.
